    A PRACTICAL DESIGN LENS

    The 4I framework

    Generative AI can make optimization easier to use. A trusted model and solver still do the rigorous work.

    01

    Insight

    Bring current, complete, consistent operating inputs into one view. This demo shows supplier capacity, demand and lane costs with their scope made explicit.

    02

    Interpretability

    Explain why a plan is feasible, how costs and service trade off, and where a shortfall comes from—in operational language.

    03

    Interactivity

    Let planners ask what-if questions, adjust priorities and compare alternatives without rebuilding the model by hand.

    04

    Improvisation

    Adapt to a disruption, update model inputs and re-optimize quickly—with people reviewing changes before action.

    Good division of labor: language models can help translate and explain; mathematical optimization enforces the encoded constraints. This prototype uses an exact min-cost-flow solver for the allocation, and a transparent rule-based assistant unless you connect a model.
